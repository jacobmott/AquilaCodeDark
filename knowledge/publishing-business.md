## Answers already established

**An LLC is optional for publishing, not a prerequisite for making the game.**
Steam permits individual rightsholders, Google personal accounts can monetize, and Apple allows individual enrollment.
Operating individually can still involve business, tax and verification duties.
Account category must reflect the actual publisher; commercial activity does not automatically require an LLC.
[Evidence and limits](native-release-direction.md#company-formation-is-optional-round-43).

**Current development and device testing can precede company and store setup.**
An ordinary Google login is different from a Play Console publisher account.
