## Current combat direction

[Combat design](../../knowledge/combat-design.md) separates confirmed requirements from proposals.
Deliberate battles allow tactics, with explosive impacts. Capable starter ship begins at its lowest offensive/defensive level.
Ordinary weapons need meaningful projectile travel. Instant long-range attacks belong only in future rare-equipment notes with major cooldowns.
Starter direction: two basic kinetic turrets, left/right, and one simple shield absorbing a few hits.
Future defenses: stronger/sustained/multiple shields, shieldless special armor, countermeasures and upgradeable automatic evasion.
Guidance can fail. Exact lost-lock behavior, starter evasion and shield recharge remain open.
Turrets have finite physical mounts and individual upgrades. Around thirty mature mounts and a year of play are illustrative, not fixed commitments.

Accepted families: autocannons, heavy kinetic guns, flak/point defense, rockets, guided missiles, torpedoes, disruption rounds and siege warheads.
Each must visibly fire cinematically, including basic guns. Autocannons need readable traveling streams.
Representative visible bullets are acceptable for performance. Levels can increase spectacle; starter effects must already satisfy.
Some weapon/defense configurations must be fully protected: unsuitable attacks can deal genuinely zero damage.

Agreed principles: visible launch/travel/contact; preserve streams and threats before secondary particles; graphics settings never change combat.
Separate attack mechanism, penetration and damage from defense compatibility/protection. Resolve the layer struck; show blocked hits clearly.
Avoid hidden chip damage through complete protection. Higher damage or fire rate alone need not defeat incompatible defenses.
Automated weapons favor known effective options and report when none are fitted. Discovery details remain open.
Exact matchups, effects, thresholds, targeting rules and capacity/turret/equipment upgrade tracks remain proposals.

The prototype still uses instant pulses. Projectile replacement, new cinematic effects and defense compatibility remain unbuilt.
No game source changed this discussion. Next: first weapon/defense matchup table alongside starter encounter.
Other open choices: mount compatibility/cap, guidance reacquisition, resource complexity, repair, persistence, defeat and acquisition.
No unanswered report question.
