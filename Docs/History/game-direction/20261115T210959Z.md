## Current combat direction

[Combat design](../../knowledge/combat-design.md) separates owner requirements from recommendations.
Capable starter ship begins at its lowest offensive/defensive level. Tactical decisions take time; impacts remain explosive and satisfying.
All ordinary available weapons need meaningful projectile travel. Hitscan/effectively instant long-range attacks leave the available design.
Keep those attacks only in future rare-equipment notes, with major cooldowns. This supersedes the earlier ordinary-beam proposal.

Starter direction: two basic kinetic turrets, left and right, and one simple shield that absorbs a few hits.
Future defenses include stronger/sustained/multiple shields or shieldless special armor, including reactive armor.
Guidance can fail under countermeasures; straight flight versus last-known-position behavior remains open.
Automatic evasion is an upgrade path; starter capability and exact control rules remain undecided.
Physical turret mounts are finite, with individual upgrades. Around thirty mature-ship turrets illustrates scale, not a fixed cap.
The year-of-play example does not establish a required progression schedule.

Recommendations: impact-based damage, limited guidance/turning, physical evasion, mount arcs, automatic fire allocation and loadout tradeoffs.
Proposed families: autocannons, heavy kinetic guns, flak/point defense, rockets, guided missiles, torpedoes, disruption rounds and siege warheads.
Proposed upgrade tracks: ship capacity, individual turret development and installed equipment.
Open: shield recharge, lost-lock/reacquisition, mount compatibility, power/heat/ammunition complexity, repairs, persistent damage, defeat and acquisition.

The prototype still uses instant pulse hits. Replacing it with traveling kinetic rounds is the first required gameplay correction.
No game source changed this discussion. Do not claim projectile combat, pulse removal or progression is already built.
