## Current interior loop

Choose Enter bridge during free Resolute flight. Walk with WASD; Shift runs. Click to capture mouse-look, or drag/use arrows.
Escape releases the cursor and pauses. Approach the helm, then press F or choose Take helm.
Existing flight controls command the same battleship from inside; forward windows show the actual nearby universe.
F leaves the helm and holds position. C or Exterior view switches outside; Return aboard restores the previous room or helm mode.

Walk through the aft passage to Peregrine's boarding station. F or Board and launch Peregrine places the shuttle outside the starboard aperture.
Resolute stays parked while the shuttle flies. Return to hangar immediately restores its saved position/orientation and disembarks at the station.
Walk back to the bridge and resume command. This is a complete local loop, not a manual docking approach or landing animation.
Planet visits still use the existing orbital descent action; the new hangar sortie does not add atmospheric entry.

Human-scale rooms use a separate scene with the same renderer. Space renders behind open windows using the exterior's named anchors.
The passage blends between bridge and hangar origins. Own hull is hidden inside; rooms do not need an exact physical fit.
One cached three-room interior is created on first entry. Distant rooms are hidden; geometry and sign textures are disposed on teardown.
Walking has substepped wall/furniture bounds, including a barrier at open space. Controls work through HTML buttons and narrow touch layouts.
Mouse capture precedes lock requests; clicks while locked skip capture. Late grants are released after route, pause or mode changes.

Room state survives navigation and quality choices during a visit. Voyage saves still retain the capital orbital state.
Reload resumes outside the battleship; personal deck position and interior mode are not persistent saves.
Selecting another craft or resetting the voyage resets the cached walking position. Rooms and instruments remain prototype art.
Crew, combat, shields, damage, engineering and quarters remain later work.
