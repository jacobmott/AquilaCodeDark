## Limits and fixes

This is an exploration prototype, not the complete game. Surface terrain is a separate sphere, not continuous orbital geography.
Walking, ground landing, detailed discoveries, combat, progression, missions and multiplayer remain unimplemented.
Two neighboring ship models supply depth references but have no AI or ship collision system.
The desktop runtime/package, physical phones, long-duration travel, fresh agent sessions and real provider-generation capture remain untested.
Original Phaser/game source and assets are retained, but active routes use the new universe.
Only Three.js 0.186.0 and matching types were added; existing application dependencies were not upgraded.

Browser testing found an initial Angular view-synchronization error and cropped/oversized ship framing; both were fixed.
Two intermediate browser-script assertions needed correction: wait for overlay DOM removal and compare travel against the departure sector.
The final regression passed. Build success alone had not caught the initial browser error.
