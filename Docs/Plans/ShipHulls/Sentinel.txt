Give it a wide, blunt hammerhead bow with clipped corners. Carry the weight low in two deep cheek castings. Undercut their leading edges and recess one narrow amber sensor slit beneath the brow. From above, the shoulders step inward toward the stern. Cut a deep, square-ended notch between two squat rectangular drives. The broad bow and forked stern should make its heading clear in silhouette.

Use graphite cast metal, medium-gray ceramic cheek faces and pale worn bevels. Put the fine detail inside the stern notch, where a few heat-stained ribs catch the light. Keep the brow and top deck quiet. Reserve the brightest amber for the sensor slit.
